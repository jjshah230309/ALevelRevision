# Documents To Upload

Uploading these lets the specification audits and everything built afterwards come from primary sources rather than general web knowledge. None of this is required before completing the student information form — that can be done first, in parallel.

## Biology — AQA 7402
- Full specification PDF (7402)
- AQA Biology data sheet / data booklet (the booklet issued in the exam)
- Required practical handbook / practical endorsement (AT skills) guidance, if your school has issued one
- Your most recent marked assessments or mock papers, ideally with mark schemes
- Any written teacher feedback or progress reports

## Chemistry — AQA 7405
- Full specification PDF (7405)
- AQA Chemistry data sheet (periodic table and formulae, issued in the exam)
- Required practical handbook, if issued by your school
- Recent marked assessments or mocks, with mark schemes
- Teacher feedback or progress reports

## Physics — AQA 7408 (Astrophysics)
- Full specification PDF (7408) — specifically the Astrophysics section (3.9) plus the compulsory core (3.1–3.8)
- AQA Physics data and formulae booklet (issued in the exam; includes the constants used in Astrophysics calculations)
- Required practical handbook, if issued
- Recent marked assessments or mocks, with mark schemes
- Teacher feedback or progress reports

## Mathematics — Pearson Edexcel 9MA0
- Full specification PDF (9MA0)
- The Mathematical Formulae and Statistical Tables booklet (the exam-room booklet — knowing what it does *not* include, e.g. the quadratic formula and sine/cosine rules, matters as much as what it does)
- The current Large Data Set release (used for the Statistics content in Paper 3)
- Recent marked assessments or mocks, with mark schemes
- Teacher feedback or progress reports

## Format

PDFs, or photos/scans of paper documents, are both fine. If something isn't available, say so on the student information form rather than leaving it blank — verified official web sources will be used instead, clearly flagged as unconfirmed against your own paperwork until you can supply it.
